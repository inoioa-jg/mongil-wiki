# Flare

Fire Destroyer primarily used as a low-field-time sub-DPS/support. Her Special
summons a Fire dragon for 233.8% damage on a 12.1s cooldown. Her Ultimate deals
504.8% damage, begins Dragon's Ambush, repeatedly applies Fire Affliction, and
marks targets to reduce DEF. Her normal support rotation is Special and then
immediate swap, with Ultimate used when available.

Version 1.4 includes balance adjustments for Flare. The exact post-patch live
damage values have not yet been transcribed here, so the displayed values above
remain a pre-1.4 reference until verified in game.

At owned A3F0 (`in_game`, user-provided, 2026-09-30):

- A1 increases Dragon's Ambush to 12 strikes and Dragon's Mark DEF reduction
  to 15%.
- A2 raises Dragon's Breath to a 14% Fire DMG boost for 10s and grants the
  same effect to all teammates.
- A3 grants all skills +2 levels and Tag-out Skill DMG +6%.

A2 is the account-relevant support breakpoint. A3 improves Flare's personal
damage but does not add another team amplifier. Re-test her against the current
Angel / Summer Verna / Vivian Fire record team only if a seat can be changed
without losing Vivian's stronger support package or Angel's contribution.
