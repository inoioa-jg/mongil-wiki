# Leeho

Leeho's base loop uses Prey Mark → charged Flash Spear → Tiger Hunter enhanced Basics. Special is a two-part Fire engage/net (11.8s). An ally Special triggers his off-field support dive, Fire Affliction, and Hunting Stance; switching to him then uses Blazing Flash. Prey Mark reduces Fire RES, but the base value requires in-game transcription.

Owned A1 adds Prey Mark to enemies caught by his Special net while controlled. The account's F2 signature effects remain untranscribed. A2–A6 do not apply; notably the additional 10% Fire RES reduction is A6 and must not be credited to this account.

Support rotation is intro → Special → swap, but realizing Hunting Stance/Blazing Flash adds a later tag-in and field-time cost.
