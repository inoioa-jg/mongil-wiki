# Verna: Summer Dive!

## Kit

Special releases Fire energy and is enhanced/refreshed during Flaming Will. Ultimate is a forward thrust applying Fire Affliction; during Flaming Will it adds a second displayed 735.7% hit and refreshes the state. Enhanced Switch deals 346.8%; a timed photo cancels it early into Special regardless of cooldown.

Passive Flaming Will activates from any non-Basic skill/counter, converts attacks to Fire, pauses its duration while Verna is off field, and generates Ember on Fire hits. This creates both on-field carry value and persistent swap utility.

## Owned progression

- **A1:** Flaming Will grants stacking +5% ATK (max 2) and an extracted Crit value that is missing; Ember Fire DMG becomes 4%.
- **A2:** swapping out gives +10% Support DMG for 15s, extends her off-field stay by 4s, and makes her cast Special once without cooldown.
- **A3–A6:** not owned.
- **F6:** exact signature fusion effects remain untranscribed and are a v0.1 blocker.

At A2F6 she is primarily the Fire on-field carry but retains meaningful automatic damage after swapping, increasing the value of low-field-time partners.
