# Angel

Ranged Fire DPS with a very short sub-DPS rotation: Special (Bullet Ballet, 220.8%, 11.5s) then immediate swap. Ultimate applies Fire Affliction. Wanted marks let ally damage trigger Angel's off-field support attack.

At owned A4: A1 raises post-support Switch Fire DMG Boost to 15%; A2 makes Locked and Loaded a 40% all-skill damage modifier; A3 skills +2/Support +6%; A4 reduces Wanted cooldown by 2s and makes the post-support tag-out Fire DMG Boost teamwide with +2s duration. A5/A6 do not apply.

Angel therefore contributes persistent off-field hits/Affliction plus a teamwide Fire boost at the account's actual investment, with little mandatory field time.
