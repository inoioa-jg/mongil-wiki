# Angel

Ranged Fire DPS with a very short sub-DPS rotation: Special (Bullet Ballet, 220.8%, 11.5s) then immediate swap. Ultimate applies Fire Affliction. Wanted marks let ally damage trigger Angel's off-field support attack.

At owned A6: A1 raises post-support Switch Fire DMG Boost to 15%; A2 makes
Locked and Loaded a 40% all-skill damage modifier; A3 gives all skills +2 and
Support DMG +6%; A4 reduces Wanted cooldown by 2s and makes the post-support
tag-out Fire DMG Boost teamwide with +2s duration; A5 gives all skills +2 and
Basic DMG +6%.

At A6, using a Switch or Support Skill fires a piercing Magnum Round. Entering
through her Switch makes Angel Locked and Loaded for 6s, doubles all Basic
Attack damage during that period, and adds piercing Magnum Rounds (`community`
transcription). This unlocks a short on-field burst but does not require it.

## Rotation at A6

- **Minimum-field-time sub-DPS:** Switch in after Support has triggered ->
  Special -> switch out. This preserves the established fast rotation and the
  A4 team Fire DMG buff.
- **Extended burst candidate:** Switch in -> Dash -> Basic chain -> Special ->
  switch out, fitting the Basic inside the 6s A6 window. Additional Basics are
  only worthwhile if they outperform the main carry's lost field time.

Angel therefore contributes persistent off-field hits/Affliction plus a
teamwide Fire boost at the account's actual investment. A6 creates optional
field time rather than making it mandatory.
