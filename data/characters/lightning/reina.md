# Reina

Community transcription checked 2026-07-29. Exact multipliers, durations, cooldowns, and awakening/Fate tiers remain unverified.

## Kit

- **Basic — Spear of Retribution:** four-hit Lightning combo. Hit 4 charges Lightning Energy. Holding Basic with at least two charges performs a variant attack; at six charges it consumes all charges and uses the variant's third hit for high Lightning damage.
- **Special — Thunderstorm:** five rapid Lightning thrusts and a finishing Lightning jab that charges Lightning Energy, followed by an upward Physical hit that launches enemies.
- **Ultimate — Secret Technique: Radiant Dragon:** throws the spear for Lightning damage, then deals bonus Lightning damage on landing and charges Lightning Energy.
- **Switch — Celestial Lightning Spear:** tag-in charges and stabs, detonates Lightning Energy, deals Lightning damage, and charges Lightning Energy. Tag-out deals Lightning damage and creates a Celestial Lightning Zone; allies inside gain a Lightning ATK boost based on Reina's max HP. Only one zone can exist.
- **Passive — Superconductive Body:** Super Armor damage increases based on max HP.
