# Yeonhwa

Community transcriptions checked 2026-07-29. Base-kit wording is corroborated by two independent databases; exact progression text still needs in-game transcription.

## Kit

- **Basic — Bloody Bamboo:** rapid Physical slashes. The finishing hit generates a Shadow that follows her actions for Physical damage.
- **Special — Thunder Chain Slash:** gap-closing Lightning slash; directional movement can alter the approach. It can be used again within a limited window, at the cost of extending cooldown. Community rotation testing reports that one input lets later casts complete after swapping, temporarily disabling her support skill; verify on account.
- **Ultimate — Storm Call:** nearby Lightning damage and launch, then enters Lightning Strike state. During the state, all attacks become Lightning, hits inflict Lightning Affliction, and Basic attacks generate a Shadow for bonus damage.
- **Switch/support — Seized Opportunity:** tag-in approaches and deals Lightning damage. During combat, an ally critical hit can trigger Yeonhwa's off-field slash/stab, dealing Lightning damage and inflicting Lightning Affliction. It has a chance to generate an inert Shadow. If a Shadow exists, the support trigger makes it follow her actions once before disappearing.
- **Passive — Wraith:** Crit Damage scales with Lightning DMG Boost up to a cap. Only one Shadow can exist. Holding Dodge moves Yeonhwa to the Shadow; Shadows disappear out of combat.
