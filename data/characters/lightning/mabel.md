# Mabel

Community transcription checked 2026-07-29. Multipliers below are displayed level-1 values. Missing percentages in the source remain `unknown`.

## Kit

- **Basic — Sanctification, Condemnation:** four-hit Lightning sequence. The fourth hit grants Epigram's Will; the second hit can generate leftover-page bonus Lightning damage and Will. Pausing after the fourth hit also grants Will. At maximum Will, holding Basic consumes all stacks and enters **Judgment**. Judgment persists through swaps, ends on timeout or Dodge, and successful Evasion Counters reset its duration. During Judgment, Sanctification targets enemies ahead; each attack adds Condemnation, increasing Neutralization damage. Displayed multipliers: 24.2% / 28.1% / 65.0% / 91.0%; leftover hit 28.1%; Sanctification combo 663.0%; finisher 117.0%.
- **Special — Doctrine, Preach:** releases Lightning energy, grants Will, and leaves energy that strikes nearby enemies again. Pausing after use grants Will. 297.0% initial + 297.0% bonus; 8.2s cooldown.
- **Ultimate — Injustice, Exterminate:** forward Lightning attack that inflicts Lightning Affliction. Mabel receives damage reduction while riding the arbiter's hand and can aim the attack. 972.0% displayed damage; source displays 3.0s cooldown, which should be verified in game because the semantic meaning may be mistranscribed.
- **Switch — Judgment, Punishment:** appears near the target, deals Lightning damage, and grants Will; pausing afterward grants additional Will. 208.3% displayed damage.
- **Passive — Epigram's Will:** qualifying attacks inject mana into Epigram to generate Will. After certain attacks, delaying input produces page fragments; pressing Basic while holding them grants Will immediately and chains into Basic hit 3.

## Awakening (character duplicates)

- **A1:** entering Judgment summons part of the arbiter, dealing 125% ATK Lightning damage, pulling enemies, then dealing bonus damage with a missing source value; 10s cooldown.
- **A2:** Special/Switch grant +1 Will. During Judgment: +100% movement speed and 6% max-HP recovery each second, up to three times. Swapping during Judgment resets its duration.
- **A3:** all four skill levels +2; Basic damage +6%.
- **A4:** Ultimate restores 20% Ultimate Gauge, grants maximum Will, raises Basic damage for 14s (value missing), and reduces damage taken by 40% for 5s; Mabel descends immediately after the Ultimate.
- **A5:** all four skill levels +2; Ultimate damage +6%.
- **A6:** Special total damage +50%, grants maximum Will, and gains two uses. Judgment duration changes 5s → 3s; Sanctification interval decreases 50%, with the source reporting +11.2% total Sanctification damage.
