# Jiwon

## Account state

Owned at A0F1. Her signature artifact is **Melody Played on Heartstrings**.

At F1, using a Switch Skill grants Vitality to Jiwon and her teammates. While
Stamina is at least 90%, Vitality gains one stack per second; each stack adds
2.5% Basic Attack, Special Skill, and Ultimate Skill DMG, up to 20 stacks. The
effect lasts 30 seconds and has a 60-second cooldown.

For the owned Wind team, Jiwon's Stamina support enables Esther's dash-cancel
rotation while also letting Vitality accumulate when Esther returns to at
least 90% Stamina. Exact stack retention during repeated dash cancels requires
account testing.

Source: [Kaiden — Melody Played on Heartstrings](https://www.kaiden.gg/mongil/artifacts/melody-played-on-heartstrings/), checked 2026-08-01.
