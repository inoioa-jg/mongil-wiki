# Narae

## Kit

- **Basic — Dew-Jade Barrage:** ranged Ice damage. Enhanced hits add butterflies, Ice Affliction, pull, and Freeze.
- **Special — Dew Blankets All:** 66.2% Ice damage, 13s cooldown; grants Dew/shields to the team, Ice DMG + Special DMG to Ice teammates, and Special DMG to non-Ice ranged teammates. Community values at A0: +50% Ice DMG and +30% Special DMG to Ice; +80% Special DMG to non-Ice ranged, 14s.
- **Ultimate — Bubble Spout:** downpour and butterflies, each displayed at 506%, both applying Ice Affliction.
- **Switch — Prison of Dew:** 104.8% Ice wave and knockback.
- **Passive — Blessings of Morning Dew:** shielding Narae grants ATK and enhances Basic; after swap she remains attacking for the effect duration (community reports 8s).

## Awakening

- **A1:** any teammate applying Ice Affliction also applies 15% Ice RES reduction for 5s; Ultimate gains droplet/water-energy hits.
- **A2:** shielding Narae grants team +22% ATK for 20s and extends Special/passive enhancements by 6s; allies can generate butterflies while Narae has Dew.
- **A3:** all skill levels +2; Ultimate DMG +6%.
- **A4:** Ice allies receive +90% Ice DMG/+60% Special DMG; non-Ice ranged receive +150% Special DMG; shield scaling rises to 200% ATK.
- **A5:** all skill levels +2; Special cooldown −4.5s.
- **A6:** Special summons clone/butterfly attacks at 80% and 200% of Special damage; latter Freezes for 3s.

## Account-relevant properties

Owned A0F1 Narae requires effectively one Special input then swap. For Ellie, the verified effects are Special Skill Damage and, from F1, Basic Attack Damage while shielded (`in_game`). Do not apply the A1 Ice RES shred or A2 ATK buff to this account.
