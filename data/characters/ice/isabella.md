# Isabella

Owned A0F0 (`in_game`, user-provided, 2026-09-30).

Isabella is a ranged Ice Destroyer built around Energy, an Ultimate-created Ice
Field, Basic Attack explosions/lasers, and some damage that continues after she
switches out. Her Special projects lingering ice; hitting it with a Basic
triggers a chain explosion. Her Ultimate creates the Ice Field, modifies Basic
Attacks, restores Energy, and detonates accumulated lasers when the field ends.
Her Switch launches an Ice shell and her tag-out behavior continues firing
lasers after another character takes the field.

Her passive converts part of Crit Rate into Ice DMG and strengthens Basics
while she has sufficient Energy. Exact live scalings and her complete
Awakening table remain untranscribed.

## Release caveat

Early live community testing reports that bosses do not receive Freeze, while
parts of Isabella's passive and signature artifact appear to require a Frozen
target. Until this interaction is patched or verified otherwise, do not assume
that all advertised Freeze-dependent bonuses function in Conquest. Her owned
signature is still recorded as F0, but its boss value is provisional.

## Account hypothesis

The first controlled comparison should be Isabella / Narae A2F1 / Francis or
Summer Francis against the established Ophelia A2F1 Ice core. Narae's newly
owned A1/A2 effects materially strengthen either Ice carry, so do not attribute
an improved score to Isabella without running the same supports and equipment
conditions.
