# Brishell

## Evidence boundary

The detailed skill text below is transcribed and translated from Korean preview
screenshots (`official`). The character screenshot shows skills at **level 12+4**;
these are not level-1 multipliers. The signature screenshot shows **Fusion 5**,
not the account's owned F1 copy. Exact English-client wording, the obscured
enhanced-Basic multipliers, and complete awakening text remain `unknown`.

The build/rotation and awakening interpretations are from the maintained
community guide (`community`). They are useful planning evidence, not a
substitute for final-client text.

## Base kit

### Basic Attack - First Friend Alfred

- Swings Alfred and slams enemies ahead for Physical damage (`official`,
  translated).
- During **Interest Overload**, the Basic becomes enhanced: Alfred tracks and
  repeatedly bites the target for Earth damage (`official`, translated).
- The enhanced attack also raises all elemental resistances based on a fixed
  portion of Suppression Damage Increase, subject to a cap whose exact value is
  not legible in the source (`official`, translated; cap `unknown`).
- Level 12+4 displayed normal-hit multipliers: **75.5%, 142.6%, 136.3%,
  157.3%** (`official`).
- Level 12+4 first enhanced-hit multiplier: **250.2%** (`official`). The next
  two displayed values are partly obscured and are intentionally not recorded.

### Special Skill - Alfred Rush

- Alfred charges and bites repeatedly for Earth damage, then Brishell slams him
  down and follows with Alien Power (`official`, translated).
- Extends Interest Overload by **3 seconds**; cooldown **13.5 seconds**
  (`official`, level 12+4 screenshot).
- Displayed level 12+4 multipliers: frenzy **665.1%**, slam **200.6%**, Alien
  Power **301%** (`official`).

### Support and Switch Skills - I'll Protect You

- **Support:** when a teammate's Basic or Special hits, Alfred descends, deals
  Earth damage, and inflicts Earth Weakness (`official`, translated).
- **Switch:** swings Alfred for Earth damage. After the Support Skill triggers,
  the Switch Skill is enhanced: Alfred charges and sweeps the enemy for Earth
  damage (`official`, translated).
- Switching out during Interest Overload without spending the enhanced Basic
  fully charges the enhanced Support Skill (`official`, translated).
- Exact multipliers, Earth Weakness magnitude/duration, and proc limits are
  `unknown` from the available screenshot.

### Ultimate - Anxiety Explosion

- Explodes Alien Power for Earth damage and inflicts Earth Weakness
  (`official`, translated).
- Displayed level 12+4 multiplier: **1,653.3%**. Displayed Earth Weakness
  duration: **5 seconds** (`official`). Its magnitude is `unknown`.

### Passive - Overloaded Attention

- Switch Skill or Ultimate grants **Interest Overload** for **6 seconds**
  (`official`, translated).
- Alien Power attacks reduce enemy DEF by **10% for 6 seconds** (`official`,
  translated). The preview's awakening summary and community guide indicate
  that applying Alien Power through the practical enhanced-Basic/support loop
  is unlocked at A1; therefore do **not** credit owned A0 Brishell with the
  guide's near-permanent DEF shred until verified in game.

## Awakening summary

- **A1:** preview summary says enhanced Basic and enhanced Support gain Alien
  Power, enabling high-uptime 10% DEF reduction (`official` summary;
  `community` interpretation). This is the support breakpoint.
- **A2:** Basics help charge enhanced Support and Basic damage increases
  (`official` summary). Community guidance treats this as her principal main-DPS
  breakpoint.
- **A4:** community guide reports DEF reduction rises from 10% to **20%**, plus
  a large personal-damage increase (`community`). Exact client text is
  `unknown`.
- **A6:** further damage and breaking improvement (`community`); exact client
  text and numbers are `unknown`.

## Signature artifact

The Korean preview title translates approximately to **Abyss/Fear from Another
World**; the exact English title is `unknown`.

At **Fusion 5**, when equipped by an Assassin (`official`, translated):

- When Suppression Damage Increase applies, ATK increases **12% for 20 seconds**.
- Support and Switch Skill damage increases **40%**.
- Using Support or Switch Skill grants **40% Earth damage for 20 seconds**,
  stacking twice; adding a stack refreshes duration.

The owned artifact is F1. Its F1 percentages are `unknown` and must not be
inferred from these F5 values. The preview also displays a Fusion-3 proficiency
bonus, but its exact translated stat relationship needs client verification.

## Community evaluation and rotation

- Suggested loop: **Special -> enhanced Basics**, then repeat or switch
  (`community`).
- Skill priority: **Basic = Support > Special > Ultimate** (`community`).
- The guide rates A0F1 Brishell above A6 Ellie as an on-field main DPS, while
  noting that Brishell has no meaningful DEF-shred support package until A1
  (`community`). This is a strong test hypothesis, not an account result.
- Ellie can still pair with Brishell as breaker/Earth buffer, particularly in
  stagger-heavy content (`community`).

## Account implications at A0F1

Owned A0F1 Brishell should first be tested as the Earth team's on-field DPS, not
described as a DEF-shred support. Candidate controlled comparisons are
Brishell / Ellie / Francis and Brishell / Ellie / Narae. Record her actual
Support/Ultimate Earth Weakness magnitude and uptime from the English client,
plus the F1 signature values, before calculating team amplification.
