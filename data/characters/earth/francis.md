# Francis

## Kit

- **Basic — This Might Pinch!:** ranged Earth combo; can generate Love Frenzy explosions.
- **Special — Extra Dose:** 210% Earth damage, Earth Affliction, 11s cooldown.
- **Ultimate — An Exciting New Trial!:** Earth impact/Affliction and an Experimental Drug Field; allies attacking inside heal from Francis's ATK.
- **Switch — Outpatient Check-up:** tag-out grants On-the-Fly Diagnosis. It does not stack with other On-the-Fly Diagnosis effects.
- **Support:** triggers when an ally falls to 75% HP or lower, damages/afflicts the enemy and heals the ally based on Francis's ATK.
- **Passive — Preventative Care:** exact full text/value still needs in-game transcription.

## Awakening

- **A1:** Special applies Elixir of Love for 3s.
- **A2:** Special grants Francis On-the-Fly Diagnosis; Love Frenzy applies Earth Affliction during it.
- **A3:** all skill levels +2; Special cooldown −4.5s.
- **A4:** On-the-Fly Diagnosis ATK Boost increases to 35%; taking a hit during it heals 10% of Francis's ATK over 5s.
- **A5:** all skill levels +2; remaining secondary effect `unknown`.
- **A6:** community analysis reports an Ultimate/Special sequence applies 30% Elemental Weakness damage amplification; exact client text/duration requires transcription.

## Rotation properties

Owned A6 Francis has approximately one second of required field time: queue Special, then swap. The ATK buff persists after further swaps (`community`). Her buff conflicts with Summer Francis's On-the-Fly Diagnosis (`in_game`).
