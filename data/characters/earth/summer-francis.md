# Francis: Summer Dive!

Special grants team Summer On-the-Fly Diagnosis and Earth allies an Earth DMG Boost; it explicitly does not stack with other On-the-Fly Diagnosis effects. Support triggers on an ally Special, deals Earth damage/Affliction, heals the team over time from her ATK, and gives the active melee teammate Summer's Vitality. Her Switch timing can trigger Special regardless of cooldown.

At the account's A0, no awakening extensions apply. Community-transcribed A0 values (20% ATK, 30% melee attack speed, 50% Earth DMG) need in-game verification because they are missing from extracted base text. A1 broadens support triggers and improves cooldown; A2 adds stacking boss damage, but neither applies here.

This unit is Earth, not Fire. In the Fire comparison she is a cross-element generic support whose value is concentrated on melee Summer Verna; she conflicts with Francis and should not be paired with him for duplicate Diagnosis buffs.
