# Ellie

Community transcription checked 2026-07-29; displayed multipliers are level 1.

## Kit

- **Basic — Back Off!:** four-hit Earth sequence. At 3 Sugar Charge, hold Basic for Pillow Pow!, consuming all stacks. Displayed Pillow Pow! multiplier: 276.4%.
- **Special — Big Donut:** rolling multi-hit Earth projectile followed by an explosion; displayed 214.3% roll and 214.3% explosion, 11.2s cooldown.
- **Ultimate — Ultimate Sweetness:** cake impact and explosion deal Earth damage; explosion applies Earth Affliction and scatters Snacks. Displayed 620.5% + 620.5%.
- **Switch — Snack Time:** 95.3% Earth damage around Ellie and scatters Snacks.
- **Passive — Sugar Charge:** Snacks heal the collector, give Ellie Neutralization DMG and one Sugar Charge. At three stacks Ellie can Pillow Pow!. Swapping out makes Ellie **Sleepy**, during which she approaches, generates Snacks, and automatically Pillow Pows at three stacks. Pillow Pow! extends Sleepy.

## Awakening

- **A1:** Special immediately grants 3 Snacks.
- **A2:** Pillow Pow! grants all teammates +10% Earth DMG for 5s; Sleepy Pillow Pow! adds 2s Sleepy duration.
- **A3:** all skill levels +2; Basic DMG +6%.
- **A4:** Special final explosion +50%; Snacks move immediately/faster; entering Sleepy creates a Super-Sized Snack for 350% ATK Earth damage plus one Snack.
- **A5:** all skill levels +2; Special DMG +6%.
- **A6:** Pillow Pow! +20% and Super Armor; holding Basic chains to hit 4; hit 4 generates three Snacks once per 10s.

## Rotation properties

At owned A6, Ellie is both an on-field carry and persistent off-field sub-DPS. Community PC rotation: Special → Pillow Pow! → Pillow Pow! (A6) → Pillow Pow! → Special → swap. Exact animation time and automatic-bounce behavior require account testing.
