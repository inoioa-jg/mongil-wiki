# Gabi

## Kit and field cost

- **Basic — Dokkaebi Bat:** Physical chain with Earth finisher. At three Spiritual Power, hold for Crushing Blow (Earth, Petrification, damage reduction); Petrified targets take increased Crushing Blow damage. Displayed Crushing Blow: 381.3%.
- **Special — Dokkaebi Technique: Create Gold!:** 198% Earth leap/slam, 11.5s cooldown.
- **Ultimate — Dokkaebi's Domain:** Earth damage, Petrification/Affliction, and Wooden Guardian Statue. Statue enhances Basics, feeds Spiritual Power, and grants team Earth DMG and DEF boosts.
- Her damage loop is inherently on field: `(Dash → Basic) × 2–3 → Crushing Blow`. Exact duration and A0 statue team-buff values remain unknown.

## Awakening

A1 resets Special after Crushing Blow; A2 adds switch Petrification; A3 skill levels +2/Basic +6%; A4 gives Crushing Blow +50% against bosses and raises Petrification bonus to 100%; A5 skill levels +2/Ultimate +6%; A6 doubles Statue effect and Basic/Crushing Blow damage during it.

At owned A0F1, none of these awakening upgrades apply. Gabi must therefore beat A6 Ellie's lost on-field output using only her base personal damage and Statue contribution; this is a high empirical bar, not established by community tier claims.
