# Maple Odong

- **Link appearance condition:** use a Special Skill (`community`).
- **Link:** deals Earth damage equal to 40% of the equipping character's ATK
  and applies **10% Earth RES reduction for 5 seconds** (`community`).
- **Rank:** Switch Skill ATK. The published value varies by Monsterling grade;
  record the account copy's grade before assigning an exact value.

For Brishell, Maple Odong belongs in slot 1. Her Special activates the Link at
the start of her main damage loop, placing the five-second Earth RES reduction
over the subsequent enhanced Basics.
