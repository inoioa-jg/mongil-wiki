## Fire RES reduction Monsterling Links

Cinder and Duoxini's Fire RES reduction effects do not stack (`in_game`,
user-provided, 2026-08-20). Their durations can still be sequenced rather than
overlapped. In the Summer Verna / Angel / Mina rotation, Angel's extended A6
field window can separate Summer Verna's Cinder activation from Mina's Duoxini
activation, reducing the practical cost of the non-stacking rule.
