# Francis and Summer Francis

Their relevant support buffs do not stack. The exact buff names and conflict rule still need transcription from the client.

## Fire RES reduction Monsterling Links

Cinder and Duoxini's identical 10% Fire RES reduction Link bonus effects do not
stack (`in_game`, user-provided, 2026-08-20). Their durations can still be
sequenced rather than overlapped. In the Summer Verna / Angel / Mina rotation,
Angel's extended A6 field window can separate Summer Verna's Cinder activation
from Mina's Duoxini activation, reducing the practical cost of the non-stacking
rule.

## General identical-effect rule

Current account understanding: **identical Bonus Effects do not stack**, but
effects modifying the same stat can stack when they are distinct effects
(`hypothesis`, user-provided, 2026-08-20). This explains why the Odong package
can work: Maple Odong's Link applies 10% Earth RES reduction for 5s, while Swamp
Odong's Link applies 3% for 5s and its Rank can apply a separately defined
5.5-8% reduction for 10s. Preserve this as a hypothesis until verified with a
buff/debuff panel or controlled damage measurements.
