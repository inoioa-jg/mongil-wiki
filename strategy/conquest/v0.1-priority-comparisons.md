# v0.1 priority comparisons

This memo derives account-specific hypotheses from the objective character records. It is not a substitute for timed Conquest runs.

## Earth: Narae versus Gabi

Core: A6F6 Ellie / A6F6 Francis.

| Third slot | Mandatory field time | Persistent contribution | Account caveat |
|---|---|---|---|
| A0F1 Narae | One Special input, then swap | Shield; Special Skill amplification; F1 Basic amplification while shielded; continues attacking after swap | A1 RES reduction and A2 ATK buff are not owned |
| A0F1 Gabi | Multiple Basic/dash sequences to generate Spiritual Power and consume Crushing Blow | Base Wooden Guardian Statue team Earth DMG/DEF; exact values unknown | No A1–A6 boss/loop improvements are owned |

**Provisional leader: Narae.** Ellie is A6 and derives most of her value from repeated Pillow Pow! cycles. Gabi's base loop directly competes for that field time, whereas Narae delivers her account-owned support in approximately one input. Gabi remains a test candidate because her base Statue values and personal damage are not yet quantified.

### Required test

Run equal-duration Conquest trials with fixed Ellie/Francis builds. Record total score, time controlled per character, Ellie Pillow Pow! count, Gabi Crushing Blow count, and buff uptime. Do not infer the result from isolated Gabi damage screenshots.

## Fire: Leeho versus Summer Francis

Core: A2F6 Summer Verna / A4F6 Angel.

| Third slot | Mandatory field time | Persistent contribution | Account caveat |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1F2 Leeho | Intro + Special; additional tag-in to cash Hunting Stance | Fire Affliction, Prey Mark/base Fire RES reduction, support dive | The commonly cited additional 10% Fire RES reduction is A6 and not owned |
| A0F0 Summer Francis | Special/support triggers; timed Switch can cast Special regardless of cooldown | Diagnosis-family ATK support, healing, melee Vitality, Earth Affliction | A1/A2 uptime and boss-damage upgrades are not owned; Diagnosis does not stack with Francis |

**No winner yet.** Summer Francis directly targets melee Summer Verna and avoids Leeho's later Hunting Stance tag-in, but her extracted A0 coefficients are not yet verified in game. Leeho has on-element RES reduction, but this account owns only A1, not the A6 enhancement. The comparison must use team score and Summer Verna field-window damage.

### Required test

Measure the same Summer Verna rotation with each third slot. Record setup duration, Summer Verna field-window duration, RES debuff uptime, Diagnosis/Vitality uptime, and Angel support triggers. Capture the A0 Summer Francis buff panel and A1 Leeho Prey Mark value before drawing a conclusion.
